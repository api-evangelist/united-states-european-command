United States European Command (EUCOM) is one of the eleven unified combatant commands of the U.S. Department of Defense. EUCOM is responsible for U.S. military operations and security cooperation in Europe, as well as parts of North Africa and the Middle East. The command works to support NATO allies, deter aggression, and ensure regional stability through military exercises, humanitarian operations, and partnership activities.

## Note on API Availability

EUCOM does not expose public-facing REST APIs. As a unified combatant command, EUCOM's data systems operate within DoD classified and unclassified networks requiring Common Access Card (CAC) authentication. Public-facing information is available through the official website https://www.eucom.mil and the EUCOM Portal at https://portal.eucom.mil. Data and intelligence sharing occurs through DoD-controlled networks including NIPRNET and SIPRNET.
